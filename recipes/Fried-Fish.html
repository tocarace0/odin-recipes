Fried Fish

Caribbean fried fish with Arepa and salad

Fried fish is surrounded by so many memories, flavors, emotions and even temperatures, fighting with the fish bones at 5-6 years old was oddly entretaining, adding a little of lime with the crunchiness of the fish is a god's gift.

Ingredients

  • 1 Coro Coro
  • Corn Flour 200 gr
  • Cabbage 100 gr
  • Onion 100 gr

Steps

  1. Mix the corn flour with water and salt
  2. Add salt and some drops of lime to the fish
  3. Cook the arepa in a Budare and fry the fish in a pan to medium heat
  4. Prepare the salad with Cabbage and Onion
  5. Serve and enjoy
