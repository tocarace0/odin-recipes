Ossobuco Stew

Meat stew with vegetables cooked in wine

As an adult I've been passionated about cooking, I liked to experiment when I was a kid, sometimes it was good and sometimes i was told it tasted like prison's food, Ossobuco Stew found its way to my "cook me if you want to show people your skills", I like to put this with some Spaghetti/Fettuccini with Huancaina Sauce for a explosion of flavors.

Ingredients

  • Ossobuco 1 Kg
  • Onion 500 gr
  • Carrot 500 gr
  • Wine 1 Lt

Steps

  1. Fry the ossobuco 1 by one and reserve
  2. On the same pan, stir-fry the onions
  3. Once golden, add back the ossobuco, the carrot and wine
  4. Cook for 2 hours at 200
  5. Serve and Enjoy
