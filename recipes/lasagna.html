Lasagna

Layers of pasta with meat stew and Bechamel Sauce

Lasagna is one of my favorite dishes, it makes me remember the times where I used to gather with my family to
eat this dish, it is also my brother's favorite dish and we used to always cook him that because we knew how much
he liked lasagna.

Ingredients

  • Pasta Layers 500 gr
  • Meat 500 gr
  • Bechamel Sauce 400 ml
  • Ham & Cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta layers with water for 1 minute on each side
  2. Prepare the meat with some tomato, basil, salt and pepper
  3. Make the Bechamel Sause with all purpose flour, butter, milk and pepper
  4. In a glass recipient, put a layer of pasta, on each layer add meat, sause, ham and cheese
  5. Cook for 30 minutes at two fifty
